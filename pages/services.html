Our Services

Comprehensive Accounting & Consulting Solutions

All Products & Services

MALIGACC Solutions is a registered legal entity, based in Gauteng, providing a full suite of business support services and solutions majoring in Business Analysis, Project Management, Accounting and Reporting, Tax services, HR services, Advisory services, Governance support, Internal control processes, Statutory compliance services, IT and business process automation.

  1. Business Analysis – Identifying what is lacking or hindering the organisation from operating optimally with special emphasis on the organisation’s structure, controls, policies, processes and procedures; and recommending effective solutions to the identified gaps.
  2. Project Management – Steering projects from start through its lifecycle to the end, ensuring detailed project work plans, alignment with business objectives, milestone management, team coordination, and reporting project performance at each recorded milestone.
  3. Accounting and Reporting – Bookkeeping and subsidiary accounting services, management accounting and reporting, review and preparation of Financial Statements for all legal entities (IFRS, GRAP, IPSAS).
  4. Tax services – Income Tax for companies, close corporations and individuals, VAT registrations and returns and tax planning and advisory services.
  5. HR and payroll administration services – Outsourcing of full payroll, EMP201 monthly returns and submissions, EMP501 bi-annual submissions and related statutory payroll services, UIF, SDL and COIDA compliance.
  6. Advisory services – Provision of practical advice on business issues and financial matters, tailored to meet the desired organisational objectives.
  7. Governance support and risk management – Provision of expert advice on risk management and review of not only financial risks but other risk areas that can have a dramatic impact on the continuing success of an entity.
  8. Policy development and internal control processes – Drafting of policies, procedures, controls and processes from scratch, and reviewing the current ones for them to be as effective as possible.
  9. IT services and automation of business processes – Ensuring that business entities are equipped with the right software solutions, support and service that will enable them to streamline their accounting and management processes across all business functions.
  10. Statutory compliance services – Assisting in putting in place various policies and procedures designed to ensure that business entities operate within applicable laws, regulations and ethical standards, so as to avoid exposure to penalties, fines or litigation.
