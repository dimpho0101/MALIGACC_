About MALIGACC

Delivering Professional Excellence Since 1996

Our Company

Who We Are

MALIGACC is a leading accounting, tax and management consulting firm serving individuals, private and public sector corporate clients. We have established ourselves as trusted advisors across multiple sectors of the economy.

We are distinguished professionals with over 30 years of combined experience across accounting and finance, management consulting, and strategic advisory. Our portfolio includes successful engagements with major corporations, SMEs, and individual clients.

The company was founded on the principles of integrity, professionalism, and a deep commitment to delivering measurable value to our clients.

Our Mission

To provide superior accounting, tax, and consulting services that enable our clients to make informed decisions, achieve their business objectives, and enhance their financial performance.

Our Vision

To be the trusted advisor of choice for accounting and management consulting services in the region, recognized for our professional excellence and client-focused approach.
